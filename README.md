## The Premier League's East Asian Recruitment Gap

An analysis of Premier League transfer patterns, domestic league strengths and the pathways connecting Japanese and South Korean football to England's top flight, between 2016 and 2026.

### Project Overview
This project examines the underrepresentation of Asian talent in the Premier League and explores if English clubs are overlooking Japanese and South Korean players.

The analysis examines historical Premier League transfer trends, identifies Japan and South Korea as the two most promising Asian footballing nations, and profiles emerging players from the J1 League and K League 1 who could have the potential to make the step up to English football.

### Research Objectives
- Analyse Premier League signings between 2016 and 2026 to identify trends in Asian players.
- Investigate which Asian nations are the strongest domestically and on the international stage.
- Identify and profile emerging talent from the J1 League and K League 1 using statistical analysis.

### Methodology

#### 1. Asian Nations 
Identifying which Asian nations have demonstrated the ability to produce promising talents in their domestic leagues as well as showing quality on an international level.

#### 2. Transfer Analysis
Analysis of Premier League transfer activity between 2016 and 2026, examining the nationalities and originating leagues of incoming players.

#### 3. Player Scouting
Statistical analysis of selected players from the J1 League and K League 1, using positional comparisons and percentile rankings to identify promising prospects.

### Key Findings
- There is an underrepresentation of Asian players in the Premier League, with just 2.04% of all transfers since summer 2016 of Asian players.
- Direct recruitment from the J1 League and K League 1 is rare but provides the opportunity to sign players for a lower fee.
- The factors behind the underrepresentation of Asian players in the Premier League are complex, ranging form cultrual barriers to accessibility and concerns about adaptation to the physical demands of English football.

### Code 
The repository includes reusable Python scripts developed to support the analysis:
- Transfermarkt Scraper: A custom web scraper built using Python to collect historical transfer data from Transfermarkt, supporting the investigation of Premier League recruitment trends.
- Plotting Utilities: Reusable plotting functions developed to generate consistent, customised visualisations for the analysis, including radar charts and player comparison figures.
